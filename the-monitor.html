Russia: budget-rule operations

Russia’s Finance Ministry buys or sells foreign currency and gold for the National Wealth Fund each month under its budget rule. It announces a combined rouble amount and does not disclose the gold share, and the Bank of Russia may offset these trades in its own reserves, so these flows are tracked here but not added to Russia’s holdings.
